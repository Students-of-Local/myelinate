# BIO 101, Unit 3 slides: Osmosis and tonicity (synthetic eval material)

Slide 1. Osmosis
Osmosis is the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.

Slide 2. Which way water moves
Water moves from the side with less solute to the side with more solute.

Slide 3. Tonicity
Tonicity compares the solute concentration outside a cell with the solute concentration inside it.

Slide 4. Isotonic
In an isotonic solution, water moves in and out of the cell at the same rate, so the cell keeps its size.

Slide 5. Hypertonic
In a hypertonic solution, the cell gains water and swells.

Slide 6. Hypotonic
In a hypotonic solution, water enters the cell. An animal cell can swell and burst. This bursting is called lysis.

Slide 7. Plant cells
A plant cell in a hypotonic solution becomes turgid because its cell wall stops it from bursting.
